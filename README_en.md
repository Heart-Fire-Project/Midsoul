# Midnight Soul

> Last edited: v2.2 | 2026/10/10  
> [中文](https://github.com/Heart-Fire-Project/Midsoul/blob/main/README.md)

This is the datapack repository for **Midnight Soul**, an original asymmetric competitive map by Heart Fire Project!

## How to Play

You can download the map directly from [Releases](https://github.com/Heart-Fire-Project/Midsoul/releases) or go to [this page](https://www.minecraftmaps.com/52453-midnight-soul) to read the introduction and download the map.

## Wiki Site

If you want to learn more about the map, welcome to visit the [Midnight Soul Wiki](https://wiki.hfpro.dev/midsoul/)!

## Help Us

If you encounter any issues (or have suggestions) while playing, feel free to submit them in [Issues](https://github.com/Heart-Fire-Project/Midsoul/issues) or share your thoughts in [Discussions](https://github.com/Heart-Fire-Project/Midsoul/discussions)!  
You can also join the [DC Group](https://discord.gg/jxUxX283Jd) for discussions.

### Contribute to Development

Please join the [DC Group](https://discord.gg/jxUxX283Jd) and consult the mods for further information.

### Multi-language (i18n)

English support is now available in an early form: Download `Midsoul LP` from [Releases](https://github.com/Heart-Fire-Project/Midsoul/releases) and install it as a resource pack, then switch your game language to English (US).

> *Some text may not take effect immediately; re-entering the game may be required.*

In addition, the English translation project on Paratranz remains open. Please go [here](https://paratranz.cn/projects/17920) and use the passphrase `Midsoul-i18n` to apply to join the project. You may also choose to work on localization for other languages independently; just let the mods know in the group above, and your translation can be added to the language pack!
